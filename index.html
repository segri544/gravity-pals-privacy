Gravity Pals — Privacy Policy

Effective 8 August 2026 · Developer: Sadık EGRI · Contact: segri544@gmail.com

Summary

Gravity Pals collects no personal data at all. The app requests no Android permissions, has no internet access, contains no advertising and no analytics, and never asks you to create an account.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or device information. Because the app has no network permission, it is technically unable to send anything anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — unlocked levels, scores, stars, daily mission state, in-game currency and settings such as language — is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It never leaves the device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics services or crash reporters. No data is shared with anyone.

Children

Gravity Pals is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

概要
